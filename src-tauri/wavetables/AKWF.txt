akwf.bin holds Adventure Kid Waveforms (AKWF) by Kristoffer Ekstrand:
4,158 single-cycle waveforms of 600 samples each, as 16-bit little-endian samples one
cycle after another, in the order of the collection's folders and files. The stereo set
is left out. banks/banks.json says where each bank starts (in cycles) and how many it has.

Source: https://www.adventurekid.se/akrt/waveforms/adventure-kid-waveforms/
        https://github.com/KristofferKarlAxelEkstrand/AKWF-FREE
